FAQ

Questions, answered

Is there really a free plan?

Yes. Starter is free forever for teams of up to five people, with no credit card required.

Can I import from other tools?

Absolutely. Bring in projects from Trello, Asana, Notion or CSV files in a couple of clicks.

How does billing work?

You're billed per active member, monthly or yearly. Add or remove people anytime and we'll prorate it.

Is my data secure?

Your data is encrypted in transit and at rest. We're SOC 2 compliant and never train models on your content.
